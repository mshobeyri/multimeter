# Board — 15 Sep 2026 — LinkedIn organic is dead

**Status:** Decided.

**Roles (isolated):** CMO, Product, Channel, Critic.

## Question

HN parked. Founder: LinkedIn posts do not get attention anymore. What is next? Not another social post.

## Agreement

- Broadcast social (HN, LinkedIn feed) is not the growth loop for this product.
- ICP still finds tools inside VS Code and via search: “Postman alternative”, “REST Client”, “API tests in Git.”
- KPI: Marketplace installs + Send / Open as MMT. Not likes, not stars, not HN points.

## Dissent

- **Channel:** keep posting; algorithm will pick one up.
- **Critic:** more posts is the same lottery as Show HN. Stop.
- **CMO / Product:** with Critic. Move budget/time to search and in-editor, or to paid that we can measure.

## Decision

**No LinkedIn cadence. No HN. No “one more post.”**

Next 14 days, pick **one** of:

1. **Marketplace search** — listing copy/keywords/screenshots vs what people type in Extensions (REST Client, Postman, API test). This is the only free channel that sits on the ICP’s machine.
2. **Small paid test** — Google (or similar) to `mmt.dev` / Marketplace for those same queries. Cap it. Kill if no installs.
3. **Ten conversations** — people who already use REST Client or Postman at work. Not content. Ask what would make them switch.

Do not stack all three. Founder picks one.

## Kill list

- LinkedIn posting as the plan
- Paid or borrowed HN
- Reddit/Dev.to spray this week
- YouTube as a *new* production this week (existing 90s demo can sit on the site; do not film for the algorithm)

## Still the founder’s job

Say which of 1 / 2 / 3. Then we execute that one.
